🎯   Goal

TAP Portugal wants to identify Silver Miles&Go members who fly Business class and have a high customer value. These travelers are our prime candidates for a Gold upgrade or a status-match offer.

🛠️  Build Steps

  1. From the App Launcher on the top left side (grid), select Data Cloud (Data 360)

  1. When in Data Cloud, head to Segments. Click New on the right corner.

  1. Keep ‘Use a Visual Builder’ and ‘Standard Segment’ selected and click on Next.

  1. Choose Individual as the segment target and name it: Silver Business-Cabin Travelers – Gold Upgrade Targets.

  1. Keep the Publish Type and Schedule as is. Click Save.

  1. Once saved, under Attributes, look for LoyaltyTier (DBX Flight Booking) in the search bar and drag it onto the canvas.
  2. Verify the count condition to Count – At Least – 1.
  3. Add the filter LoyaltyTier Is Equal To Silver.

  1. Click AND and add the filter Cabin (DBX Flight Booking) Is Equal To Business. Click on Done on the bottom right.

  1. Now in the left panel, go to Resources > Calculated Insights > DBX Customer Value. Drag CustomerValueScore__c onto the canvas, placing it below the booking block.

  1. Set CustomerValueScore__c Is Greater Than 40.
  2. Make sure the two main blocks are joined with AND. Click Done in the bottom right corner.

  1. Once done, click Save on the top right. A green message will confirm the save and start the recount.
  2. Once finished, check your Segment Population. You should see a number: 804.

  1. Click Preview on the top right to look at the matching individuals.

🔧 Troubleshooting Common Issues

  • I can't find CustomerValueScore__c in the menu! Make sure you are looking in the right folder. It is not under standard Attributes. You must click through Resources > Calculated Insights > DBX Customer Value.
  • My population is 0 or way too high. Check the logic joining your blocks. The DBX Flight Booking block and the DBX Customer Value block must be joined by AND, not OR. Also, ensure you set the flight booking count strictly to Count – At Least – 1.

🚀 Bonus Exercise: Add Web Engagement Data

Let's make our segment even more targeted by bringing in web engagement data to find users who are actively browsing our site for specific destinations (like Rio) - so we can target long-haul high priority passengers with specific promotions.

Part 1: Add the Web Data Stream via Data Kit

  1. In Data Cloud, go to Data Streams section and click New.
  2. Choose the Data Kit option (Installed Data Kits & Packages). Click Next.

  1. On the left hand side, search for dbx and select the DBX Travel Data Kit. Once loaded, find the data bundle: DBX_Web_Engagement_Bundle. Select it and click on Next.

  1. On the next view, wait for it to load. Once loaded, make sure website_browse is selected under Available Objects. Do not change anything and click on Next.

  1. Review the objects and mapping that come with the bundle, then click Next.

  1. As the last step, click on Deploy.

  1. Wait until the data stream shows in Data Streams as Active and the first ingestion finishes (Last Run Status = Success). Once it goes back to the main Data Streams section, reload the page as it may have processed already.

Part 2: Add the New Condition to the Segment

  1. Go to Segments and open your Silver Business-Cabin Travelers – Gold Upgrade Targets segment - click on Edit Rules.

  1. In the left panel under Attributes, search for the new web object (Website Engagement) under Related Data Model Objects.

  1.  Select it and search for the attribute Page URL and add it as a filter. Set the condition to: Page URL Contains ‘rio’.

  1. Make sure this new block is joined to the others above it with an AND operator.
  2. Click Save and observe the change in the Segment Population. Adding this condition should drop your population down to 103.
